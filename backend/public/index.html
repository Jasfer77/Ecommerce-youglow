Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept payments through GCash and BPI bank transfer. Full payment is required before your order is processed and shipped or delivered.

2. What is your return and exchange policy?

Due to the nature of our products, all sales are generally final. Returns and exchanges are not accepted for change of mind. However, if you receive an incorrect, damaged, or defective item, please contact us so we can review your concern.

3. Where are you located?

We are a Cebu-based online shop serving customers throughout the Philippines.

4. Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We use Maxim for Cebu City and nearby areas, and J&T Express for deliveries outside Cebu City, subject to courier coverage and product shipping restrictions.

5. How long does delivery take?

Local deliveries may be available on the same day, depending on rider availability. Nationwide shipping times depend on your destination and the courier's delivery schedule.

6. Can I place an order without paying first?

No. Full payment through GCash or BPI bank transfer is required before we process and dispatch your order.

7. How can I track my order?

For local deliveries, we can provide available delivery updates. For J&T Express shipments, we will share tracking details when available.

8. How do I place an order?

Contact us through our official communication channels, select your desired product, provide your delivery details, and complete payment. Your order will be processed once payment is confirmed.

9. Will I receive instructions with my order?

We provide applicable product information and educational materials with our orders. These materials do not replace professional medical advice or instructions from a qualified healthcare provider.

10. Do you provide support after purchase?

Yes! We offer online customer support, educational resources, and personalised tracking materials where applicable to help you stay organised throughout your wellness journey.

11. Are your products suitable for everyone?

No. Product suitability depends on the specific product and individual circumstances. Prescription medications and injectable products require appropriate medical assessment and supervision. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional before use.
